# Personas

Scripted copies of real Pain and Gain players, built from their replays, for
playing offline with `bin/arena sim --arena season4-pain_and_gain --opponent
persona:<name>`. `--bot <saved build | persona:name>` picks the bot under test
and `--maps N` plays every match on the N newest archived maps.

| file            | what it is                                                        |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `persona.mjs`   | the parameterised bot: army waypoints, scout flag order, fighting |
| `learn.mjs`     | learns fight movement (close in, hold, back off) from replays     |
| `temik911.mjs`  | temik911's parameters, from 145 replays                           |
| `*.policy.json` | learned movement tables (generated by `learn.mjs`)                |

## Not yet trustworthy

A persona is only useful if it reproduces the real player's results against
our saved builds. temik911's does not. The real temik911 beat the mass-attack
build in 8 of 8 test games and the picket build about half the time. Every saved
build beats the persona in every simulated game, with the learned movement
and without.

The simulator's combat is not the problem. Replaying 2,700 real creep-ticks of
recorded attacks and heals through it gives exactly the real hits 96% of the
time, and is off by one or two otherwise. What the persona lacks is the
player's fight behaviour: when its creeps act, whom they shoot, whom they heal.
Until a persona passes that check, its results say nothing about real play.
